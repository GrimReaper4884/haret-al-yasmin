# Other Major Anime-Style Roblox Tower Defense Games (excluding Anime Vanguards): Comparative Mechanics Breakdown

> **Research method and confidence note (read first).** These notes were compiled on 2026-10-17. WebFetch could not reach any of the target domains: Fandom wikis, Sportskeeda, Deltia's, ProGameGuides, Pocket Tactics and Rolimons all returned DNS or egress-proxy refusals. So every finding below comes from **search-engine result summaries** of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Treat exact percentages as "as reported by the cited page at its last crawl." Many guide pages are 1-2+ years old, and Roblox TD games rebalance constantly. Figures marked **(dated)** or **(uncertain)** should not be presented as current. Where sources conflict, both are listed.

## 1. Summoning / banners / rarities / pity (per game)

### Takeaway
All of these games use a gem-currency gacha. A 50-gem single pull with a 10-pull discount is the ASTDX / Anime Reborn / Anime Adventures standard. Top-rarity rates sit in the 0.25-1% range, and an explicit pity counter is attached (for example ALS: Legendary at 60 pulls, Mythic at 250). Anime Adventures popularized hourly-rotating banners with 3 featured Mythics. ASTD used star rarities (3-6 stars, plus "Unbound") rather than named tiers.

### Cited Findings
**All Star Tower Defense X (ASTDX)**
- Single summon is 50 Gems and a 10x is 450 Gems (a 10% bulk discount). Banner borders encode star rarity: 5-star is rainbow, 4-star red, 3-star yellow — [Sportskeeda ASTDX beginner's guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-star-tower-defense-x-a-beginner-s-guide); [Deltia's ASTDX beginner guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281458)
- One guide cites a 1% chance for a specific featured 5-star on the X banner. "Unbound" characters are cited at 0.01% on both banners **(uncertain, low-quality aggregator)**. Pity guarantees a 5-star or higher unit — same sources as above.
- The original ASTD Trials reward a 5-star unit, a 6-star unit, or an **orb**. Orbs boost a specific unit; for example, the Quake Orb raises Rukia's initial damage on her 6-star form — [ASTD Fandom: Trials](https://allstartd.fandom.com/wiki/Trials)
- A Trello card shows orbs modifying 6-star stats, e.g. "Range - 28 (33 with orb)" — [Trello Finder: ASTD](https://trellofinder.com/games/all-star-tower-defense)

**Anime Last Stand (ALS)**
- One banner version is reported as 2x Mythic (0.5%), 1x Legendary (2%), 2x Epic (27.5% each) and 2x Rare (70% each). Pity is Legendary at 60 summons and Mythic at 250 summons. A wiki copy shows different numbers: 1x Mythic 0.5%, 2x Rare 35% each, Mythic pity 100, and "Jewel" rather than "Emerald" costs **(conflicting, version-dependent)** — [ALS Fandom: Summoning Banners](https://alsroblox.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Banners); [alternate wiki copy](https://anime-last-standfakefakefake.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Banners)
- An older guide gives the same 60 / 250 pity and says each summon costs 20k gems, or 16k with VIP **(dated; the currency appears to have been changed later)** — [GINX TV: ALS summon chances & pity](https://www.ginx.tv/en/roblox/anime-last-stand-summon-chances-pity-system)
- A wiki heading references a "Secret Unit (500 Pity)" system, with no published odds — [ALS Fandom: Summoning Banners](https://alsroblox.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Banners)

**Anime Adventures (AA)**
- Each summon costs 50 Gems (40 with the VIP gamepass) or a Summon Ticket. **The banner refreshes every hour with random units, featuring 3 Mythicals** — [AA Fandom: Summon](https://animeadventures.fandom.com/wiki/Summon)
- Base Mythic chance is 0.25% on the Special Banner and 0.5% on the Event Banner. Secret units are about 1 in 400,000 (non-shiny around 0.005%). An older forum figure gives 0.562% for any Mythic and 0.231% for the featured Mythic **(conflicting)** — [AA Fandom: Summon](https://animeadventures.fandom.com/wiki/Summon); [Vintage Is The New Old FAQ](https://www.vintageisthenewold.com/faq/what-are-the-odds-of-getting-a-secret-unit-in-anime-adventures)
- Pity is a percentage meter. Each summon adds 2% Legendary pity and 0.25% Mythic pity, and 100% guarantees a unit of that rarity. Luck Potions raise Mythic and Legendary odds but do not reduce pity. Shiny variants exist, and a "Shiny Hunter" gamepass raises shiny odds — [Games Learning Society](https://www.gameslearningsociety.org/how-much-gems-to-get-mythical-in-anime-adventure/); [AA Fandom: Summon](https://animeadventures.fandom.com/wiki/Summon); [AA Fandom shiny-mythic thread](https://animeadventures.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000131206)
- Weekly achievements give about 1,700 gems per week and reset at UTC midnight Monday-Tuesday **(dated, about 2 years old)** — [Games Learning Society](https://www.gameslearningsociety.org/how-much-gems-to-get-mythical-in-anime-adventure/)

**Anime Reborn**
- A single summon is 50 Gems and a 10x is 500 Gems (no bulk discount) at the Summon Station. Units come without traits — [Destructoid: Anime Reborn traits](https://www.destructoid.com/anime-reborn-traits-tier-list/); [ProGameGuides: Anime Reborn traits](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-traits-in-anime-reborn-roblox/)

**Anime Rangers X / Re:Rangers X**
- Featured secret units such as Shadow Knight are cited at roughly 0.1-0.2% per summon. Tanjiro is cited at 1%, with about 5,000+ "Ranger Crystals" recommended. Fire Fist is obtainable only in **AFK World, at a 0.5% chance every 15 minutes** (an AFK-reward mechanic) **(uncertain, varies by guide)** — [Deltia's: ARX secret units](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=206188); [Deltia's: How to get Tanjiro](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=204852)

**Anime Defenders**
- Has "Secret" units above Mythic — [Sportskeeda: Secret units in Anime Defenders](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/what-secret-units-anime-defenders)
- A special-summon currency called **Wishes** was introduced; one Wish was sold at 249 Robux, which was criticized as excessive (see §7) — [ProGameGuides: dev promises improved F2P](https://progameguides.com/roblox/after-negative-fan-reactions-roblox-developer-promises-improved-f2p-experience/)

### Inferences
- The genre has converged on a small set of conventions: a 50-gem single pull, a Mythic/Secret top tier below 1%, a visible pity counter, and VIP gamepasses that discount pulls (AA 50 to 40; ALS 20k to 16k).
- AA's hourly-rotating banner with 3 featured Mythics creates a "check back every hour" habit loop. Later games mostly moved to fixed or update-length banners.
- ASTD's star system (3-6 stars, Unbound) and 6-star-plus-orb upgrades are the genre's oldest rarity framing. Later games switched to named rarities (Rare / Epic / Legendary / Mythic / Secret).

### Gaps
- Current (2026) exact rates and pity for Anime Defenders, Anime Crusaders and Re:Rangers X standard banners were not found.
- ASTDX pity count was not found; the guides only say "pity exists."
- Anime Adventures' post-December 2024 relaunch banner structure was not verified (the wiki figures may be pre-takedown).
- Unverified recollection, not sourced: ASTD originally sold multiple banners (standard and "Limited"), and 6-star units were obtained by evolving 5-stars with orbs and items. Do not state this as fact without a source.

## 2. Traits / rerolls / percentages (per game)

### Takeaway
All of these games use a per-unit trait slot rerolled with a dedicated consumable: ASTDX Trait Burners, ALS Technique Shards, Anime Defenders Trait Crystals, Anime Reborn Shards. The top trait typically sits at 0.03-0.2% and gives an outsized multiplier, often paired with a **placement limit of 1**. Several games added **trait pity** (ASTDX: Corrupted pity 450, All Star pity 1000; Anime Reborn: a Mythic/Secret trait every 200 rolls).

### Cited Findings
**ASTDX (rerolled with Trait Burners, 1 per roll; odds viewable via the Index button in the Robot Area)**
- **All Star 0.2% (pity 1000)**, **Genesis 0.7%** (+12.5% DMG, -8.5% SPD, +8.5% RNG, +50% True Damage; one source says it ignores half the Elemental rules), **Corrupted 0.8% (pity 450)** (-10% SPD, +7.5% RNG, +20% Shield Bypass, marks enemies for +10% damage). Common traits such as Tank are around 12%. Bloodlust, Zenkai, Dexterity and Perception also exist — [ASTDX Fandom: Traits](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Traits); [Beebom ASTDX trait tier list (Mar 2026)](https://beebom.com/all-star-tower-defense-x-traits-tier-list/); [Destructoid ASTDX trait tier list](https://www.destructoid.com/all-star-tower-defense-x-trait-tier-list/); [BloxInformer ASTDX Traits](https://bloxinformer.com/wikis/all-star-tower-defense-x/traits/)
- Trait Burner shop prices are 299 Robux for 12 and 3,200 Robux for 180. Burners also come from codes and gameplay. A gem gamble ("Royal Drops") gives an 11% chance for 50 gems. **Traits apply in every mode except PvP, Classic Infinite and Strategist Trials** — [Destructoid ASTDX trait tier list](https://www.destructoid.com/all-star-tower-defense-x-trait-tier-list/)
- Note: ASTDX has **Shield Bypass and True Damage stats and "Elemental Rules"**, which implies enemy shields and an elemental-matchup system — same sources as above.

**Anime Last Stand ("Techniques," rolled at the lobby's Technique Guy using Technique Shards; the old technique is replaced, with no guarantee of an upgrade)**
- **Glitched 0.03%**: +750% DMG, +35% Range, -15% SPA, +60% money, +75% crit damage, +15% crit chance, **one placement only**. Another source lists Glitched at +350% DMG with a 2-placement limit **(patch-dependent conflict)**. **Avatar 0.1%** (550% DMG in one source, +350% in another). **Shinigami 0.2%**. The Scoped tiers are I 12%, II 9% and III 4% — [Deltia's ALS traits list](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=168829); [ProGameGuides ALS techniques](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-traits-in-anime-last-stand-als/); [ALS Fandom: Techniques](https://alsroblox.fandom.com/wiki/Techniques)
- **Double traits** (two techniques on one unit) are around 0.5% per roll — [Deltia's: Double Traits in ALS](https://deltiasgaming.com/how-to-get-double-traits-in-anime-last-stand-roblox/)

**Anime Defenders (rerolled with Trait Crystals in the Summoning Area; the new trait always replaces the old)**
- Brawler 1 about 7.95% (7.8% for Brawler 3 in one list), Critical 3 6%, **Prodigy 10%** (XP boost: 10% per Gamerant vs 50% per ProGameGuides and Sportskeeda), **Bullseye 1 2.5%**, **Midas Touch 1 1.5%** (money), **Precision 1 0.8%**, **Almighty 1 0.1%** (about 15% cooldown reduction and +10% range; descriptions vary) — [Gamerant: Anime Defenders all traits](https://gamerant.com/roblox-anime-defenders-all-traits/); [Escapist](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/every-trait-in-anime-defenders-listed/); [ProGameGuides (Update 7)](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-traits-in-anime-defenders-roblox/); [Sportskeeda trait tier list](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/anime-defenders-trait-tier-list)
- **Divine Traits** are a second, higher trait layer. They need a separate **Divine Trait Crystal** bought from the **Raid Shop with Raid Shards**. Regular Trait (Reroll) Crystals drop from medium- and hard-difficulty maps — [Try Hard Guides: Divine Traits](https://tryhardguides.com/anime-defenders-divine-traits-guide/); [Anime Defenders Fandom: Crystals](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/Crystals); [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-traits-in-anime-defenders-roblox/)
- A 2026 guide still advises spending Trait Crystals only on SS- and S-tier units — [BloxGuides Anime Defenders 2026](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/anime-defenders)
- **(Dated: most Anime Defenders trait pages are 700+ days old.)**

**Anime Reborn (Trait Reroll NPC in the lobby, 1 Shard per reroll)**
- Pity gives a Mythic or Secret trait every 200 rolls. Warrior I is 13.1% (+5% DMG). Ethereal is 1.4% in one source and 0.4% in another **(conflict)**. **Paladin** (Secret) is 0.2% for +100 DMG and +10% Range. **Miracle** gives damage, range and a random buff, limited to **one unit per field**. The S-tier traits are Demon, Ethereal, Heavenly, Miracle, Paladin and Slayer — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/anime-reborn-traits-tier-list/); [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-traits-in-anime-reborn-roblox/); [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-reborn/traits)

**Anime Rangers X (trait center in the main plaza; each roll gives a main trait plus a very low chance of a sub-trait)**
- Sniper gives +15% range, Tank +15% HP, Investor 10% off summon and upgrade costs. **Seraph** gives +50% DMG, HP and Range and **+1 spawn cap** (more placements). **Capitalist** halves summon and upgrade costs. One trait **duplicates the summoned unit**. Units have HP, which implies enemies can attack units. Top meta pairings are Capitalist, Sovereign and Violent. Roll percentages were not published by the sources found — [Pocket Tactics: ARX traits](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-rangers-x-traits); [Pocket Tactics: ARX tier list](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-rangers-x-tier-list); [astucejeuxps4 (FR)](https://astucejeuxps4.com/anime-rangers-anime-x-liste-et-guide-des-traits/)

**Anime Crusaders**
- In the newer list, Fierce, Agile and Focus are common. **Radiant is 0.2%** (+27.5 DMG, +15% Range, -15% attack cooldown). Biohazard is also meta. An older list cites "Crusader" at +300% DMG **(conflicting; probably patch-dependent)** — [BloxInformer: Anime Crusaders Traits](https://bloxinformer.com/wikis/anime-crusaders/traits/); [Destructoid Anime Crusaders tier list](https://www.destructoid.com/anime-crusaders-tier-list/)

### Inferences
- **The genre's trait template:** commons around 8-13% for a +5-15% single stat; mid tiers around 1-4%; a money or "Midas/Capitalist" trait as an economy-trait category; a near-mythic 0.1-0.8% trait; and a "god trait" at 0.03-0.2% with an enormous multiplier offset by a 1-placement limit. That limit is the key balancing lever that stops a god trait from trivializing content when stacked.
- **Trait pity counters** (ASTDX 450 / 1000, Anime Reborn 200) were added in later games to reduce frustration. Anime Vanguards' Monarch pity (1,500 rerolls, per the earlier search) follows the same pattern.
- **Layered traits** (Anime Defenders' Divine Traits; ALS and ARX double or sub-traits) are the main way these games extend the reroll sink after players hit a god trait.
- Traits being disabled in PvP and Classic Infinite (ASTDX) shows the "fair mode" design pattern: competitive and leaderboard modes strip RNG power.

### Gaps
- Complete current trait tables, with percentages summing to 100%, could not be fetched for any game.
- ALS "Monarch": no source confirmed a Monarch technique in ALS. The Monarch figures found (0.1%, 1,500-roll pity) belong to Anime Vanguards. Do not attribute Monarch to ALS without verification.
- Stat-reroll systems separate from traits were not found for these games in the sources accessed. Anime Vanguards is known for stat rolls; whether ALS, ASTDX or others have per-stat grades or "potential" rerolls was not verified.

## 3. Units, evolutions / awakenings, abilities, placement

### Takeaway
Evolution is a key progression sink. ALS gates evolutions behind mode-specific items (raid and event shop items, Godly Trials Glyphs plus an empty Helix). ASTD uses 5-star to 6-star upgrades with orbs. AA has a small number of evolvable Mythics. Placement caps are handled per unit and per trait; god traits limit placements to 1, while Seraph raises the cap.

### Cited Findings
- **ALS evolutions:** items come from Raid Shop and Summer Breach Shop tokens, Halloween Shop Cookies, the Boss Rush Shop (e.g. Elven Bracelet), and drops such as Spirit Shards and Radiant Crystals. **Godly evolutions** require completing quests to get a **Glyph**, then combining it with an **empty Helix** to craft the evolution item. These **Godly Trials are only in World 2**. A progression guide says World 1's goal is finishing the **Guide Book chapters** to unlock the World 2 portal, with "evolve Idol and Hero Hunter" on the checklist and "Mythic Spirit Shots" as an evolution resource — [ALS Fandom: Godly Trials](https://alsroblox.fandom.com/wiki/Godly_Trials); [ALS Progression Guide](https://www.anime-last-stand.wiki/guide/anime-last-stand-progression-guide); [XYUltra ALS evolution guide](https://xyultra.com/anime-last-stand-evolution-guide/); [Try Hard Guides: Evolve Flash](https://tryhardguides.com/how-to-evolve-flash-in-anime-last-stand/)
- One page lists identical evolution costs for every Legend unit (100 Legendary Shards plus 500,000 Gold) **(unreliable; conflicts with other sources)** — [animelaststand.wiki: Legend Stages](https://animelaststand.wiki/story-mode-and-progression/legend-stages/)
- ALS evolution walkthroughs exist per unit, e.g. Red Pirate (Conqueror), a Shanks-based unit, as of Update 63 — [Deltia's: Red Pirate (Conqueror)](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=295472)
- **ALS has money/farm effects inside traits**: Glitched includes +60% money — [Deltia's ALS traits](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=168829)
- **AA evolutions:** an undated source says four Mythic units could evolve, all obtained via the gacha — [Games Learning Society](https://www.gameslearningsociety.org/how-much-gems-to-get-mythical-in-anime-adventure/)
- **ASTDX unit upgrades:** **Skill Nodes** cost several kinds of **Skill Orbs** plus unit duplicates. There is an **Ascension** system (e.g. Explosion Artist) — [Deltia's ASTDX beginner's guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281458); [Sportskeeda: Explosion Artist ascension](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/explosion-artist-all-star-tower-defense-x-how-get-ascension-stats)
- **ARX:** units have HP (Tank trait), and Seraph adds +1 spawn cap — [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-rangers-x-traits)
- **ASTD 6-star orbs** change base stats (e.g. range 28 to 33) — [Trello Finder](https://trellofinder.com/games/all-star-tower-defense)

### Inferences
- Evolution items are deliberately spread across *every* mode (raids, events, boss rush, trials). This is the core retention tool: each mode exists to feed an evolution item, so players have to engage with all of them.
- Gating the strongest units behind world progression (ALS World 2 Godly Trials) also acts as a soft tutorial.
- Duplicates as an upgrade cost (ASTDX nodes) give extra gacha pulls value beyond the first copy.

### Gaps
- Ultimate / active ability implementation (manual vs automatic activation, cooldowns, cutscenes) was **not found** for any of these games in accessible sources.
- Hill vs ground placement rules and dedicated farm/money units (beyond money traits) were not verified per game.
- Maximum unit placement totals per game were not found.
- Unverified recollection, not sourced: ASTD and AA units commonly had activatable "abilities" with cooldowns, and ASTD had hill/ground placement distinctions. Treat as unverified.

## 4. Modes and reward structures

### Takeaway
The mode stack has become standard: Story, then Infinite, then Raids, Challenges, Legend Stages, Portals, Trials or Towers, Boss events, and AFK. AA's **Infinity Castle** (100 rooms × 15 waves, seasonal) and ASTD's **Trials** (which reward 5- and 6-star units and orbs) are signature innovations. ASTD and ASTDX also have **PvP**, which is rare in the genre.

### Cited Findings
- **AA Infinity Castle:** 100 rooms, each with 15 waves, and the castle changes each season — [Gamer Journalist: Infinity Castle](https://gamerjournalist.com/how-to-complete-infinity-castle-in-anime-adventures-how-many-rooms/)
- **AA Update 2 (Aug 1, 2022):** new map, new units, level cap raised to 70, event quests and lag improvements — [Prima Games](https://vip-develop.primagames.com/?p=63170)
- **AA Infinite Mode** is an unlockable mode — [Prima Games: unlock Infinite](https://vip-develop.primagames.com/tips/how-to-unlock-infinite-mode-in-anime-adventures)
- **ASTD Trials** reward a 5-star unit, a 6-star unit, or an orb — [ASTD Fandom: Trials](https://allstartd.fandom.com/wiki/Trials)
- **ASTDX modes include PvP, Classic Infinite and Strategist Trials** (all trait-disabled), plus Infinite Wave (Classic) — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-star-tower-defense-x-trait-tier-list/); [Deltia's ASTDX Infinite Wave (Classic) guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=296507)
- **ASTDX Update 6 (Mar 21, 2026)** added a **battle pass, a Portals mechanic and 13 new units** — [RoWatcher: ASTDX](https://rowatcher.com/games/6057699512/all-star-tower-defense-x)
- **ALS Raids** arrived in Update 5 Part 1, with 6 stages of 20 waves each. Clearing Act 6 of maps can grant evolution items or a unit. ALS also has **Portals** (special challenges with unique rewards), **Legend Stages** (unlocked after Story), **Godly Trials** (World 2) and Boss Rush (via its shop) — [ALS Fandom: Raids](https://alsroblox.fandom.com/wiki/Raids); [ALS Fandom: Guides](https://alsroblox.fandom.com/wiki/Guides); [animelaststand.wiki: Legend Stages](https://animelaststand.wiki/story-mode-and-progression/legend-stages/)
- **Anime Defenders** has Raids (Raid Shards feed the Raid Shop, which sells Divine Trait Crystals) and a **Hall of Mirrors** mode: survive 30 minutes on the hardest difficulty for a chance at Wishes — [Try Hard Guides](https://tryhardguides.com/anime-defenders-divine-traits-guide/); [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/after-negative-fan-reactions-roblox-developer-promises-improved-f2p-experience/)
- **ARX AFK World:** passive rolls of 0.5% every 15 minutes for a specific unit (Fire Fist) — [Deltia's: ARX secret units](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=206188)

### Inferences
- Every mode is paired with a dedicated shop currency (Raid Shards, Boss Rush tokens, event Cookies) that buys evolution items or trait consumables. This "mode, then currency, then shop, then power" loop is the genre's economic backbone.
- Long survival modes (Infinity Castle, Hall of Mirrors, Infinite) double as leaderboard and endgame tests. Disabling traits in some of them (ASTDX) keeps leaderboards skill-based.
- AFK worlds (ARX) raise concurrent-player numbers on the CCU charts and add a passive-reward loop.

### Gaps
- Reward tables (exact drop rates per stage or act) were not retrievable.
- PvP details for ASTDX (format, rewards) were not found.

## 5. Enemy and boss design patterns

### Takeaway
Genre-specific enemy data for these anime TDs was thin in the accessible sources. The strongest evidence is indirect. ASTDX traits reference **Shield Bypass**, **True Damage** and **Elemental Rules**, and ARX units have **HP** (so enemies attack units). Adjacent Roblox TDs document boss stun immunity, shields, rage phases, and bosses that roam, stun or "delete" units.

### Cited Findings
- ASTDX Corrupted gives +20% Shield Bypass, so enemies carry shields. Genesis gives True Damage that "ignores half the Elemental Rules," which implies an element or affinity system — [ASTDX Fandom: Traits](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Traits); [Beebom](https://beebom.com/all-star-tower-defense-x-traits-tier-list/)
- In Anime World Tower Defense (a comparable anime TD), Super and Ultra Bosses move anywhere on the map and can stun, delete or absorb units, drain stamina and hit your base. **Ultra Bosses have a time limit** — [Anime World TD Fandom: Enemy Types](https://anime-world-tower-defense-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Enemy_Types)
- Shields absorb damage separately from HP, and some bosses bypass unit shield HP — [Roblox World TD Fandom: Traits](https://roblox-world-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Traits)
- Genre reference from Tower Defense Simulator: all bosses have stun immunity, and "stuns" become slows (20% for bosses, 10% for bosses with immunity). Some bosses use full-immunity shields and rage modes — [TDS Fandom: Stun Immunity](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Stun_Immunity); [TDS Fandom: Boss](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Boss)

### Inferences
- The likely shared pattern for the anime TDs is: shields (countered by shield bypass or true damage), elemental resistances, boss stun immunity, and unit-disabling boss skills (stun or delete) that make unit HP and anti-stun traits meaningful.

### Gaps
- Specific enemy rosters, armor, regen, summoner, flying and stealth/hidden mechanics for ALS, AA, Anime Defenders, ASTDX, Reborn, ARX and Crusaders were **not found** in accessible sources.
- Boss phase design and cutscenes were not found.

## 6. Economy and monetization

### Takeaway
Monetization is consistent across the genre: VIP passes (cheaper summons), speed passes (3x game speed), extra team slots, starter packs, premium battle passes around 699 Robux, and direct sales of reroll consumables (ASTDX Trait Burners 299 R$ for 12). Selling summon currency directly at a high price (Anime Defenders Wishes at 249 R$ each) caused backlash.

### Inferences
- Pricing the reroll consumable rather than units (ASTDX burners) monetizes the long tail after unit collection is complete.

- Trading (AA, Anime Defenders) boosts engagement and the "value list" economy, but invites grey-market RMT and puts pressure on rarity scarcity.

### Inferences
- **IP risk is the biggest existential threat.** Two of the largest anime TDs (AA in 2023 and ARX in 2025) were taken down. AA's case was a DMCA by a licensee; ARX's cause was not confirmed. Both relaunches recovered only partially (Re:Rangers X peaked at about half of ARX). Lesson: use original or parody characters ("Red Pirate", "Hero Hunter", "Idol" in ALS; "Shadow Knight", "Solar Fist", "Oldfang Reaver" in ARX). Several of these games already obfuscate names for this reason.
- **Launch hype decays fast.** Anime Defenders hit 378k three months after launch, but P2W monetization of summon currency (249 R$ Wishes behind a 30-minute hardest-difficulty grind) triggered backlash.
- **Longevity comes from cadence.** ALS (60+ updates, seasonal passes) and ASTD (since 2020/21, still about 10-23k CCU, then rebooted as ASTDX) outlasted bigger launches through steady updates and many mode-linked evolution sinks.
- **Sequels and reboots** (ASTDX, Re:Rangers X, AA's relaunch) are a common response to tech debt or IP problems, but they split the community and rarely beat the original's peak.

## 8. Signature innovations, by game (summary for the report writer)

### Takeaway
Each game is remembered for one or two systems. ASTD: star rarities, 6-star orbs, Trials and PvP. AA: hourly 3-Mythic banners, shinies, trading, Infinity Castle, and the full mode ladder. Anime Defenders: a huge launch, Divine (second-layer) traits, and Wishes. ALS: Techniques with a 0.03% one-placement god trait, double traits, mode-sourced evolutions, and Godly Trials / World 2. ARX: unit HP, spawn-cap and duplication traits, and AFK World. ASTDX: trait pity, Shield Bypass / True Damage / elemental rules, and trait-free fair modes. Anime Reborn: a 200-roll trait pity. Crusaders: a newer entrant at mid-scale.

### Inferences
- For a new game blueprint, the proven "must-have" set is: a 50-gem gacha with visible pity; a trait slot with a consumable reroll, trait pity, and a 1-placement god trait; mode-linked evolution items; Infinite, Raid, Legend, Portal and Challenge modes; a battle pass plus speed and VIP passes; and original or parody IP to avoid takedowns. Options that differentiate: PvP or fair modes with traits disabled, AFK worlds, second-layer traits, and roguelike tower climbs (Infinity Castle).
