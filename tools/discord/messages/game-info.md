# 🎮 Haret Al-Yasmin · حارة الياسمين

A co-op **tower-defense** game on Roblox. Collect the lane's defenders, team up with up to 3 friends, and hold the lane gate against thieves, gendarmes and rival-lane thugs.
🧪 The game is in testing — the release date will be posted in #announcements.

**🗺️ Modes**
• Episode 1 · Night of the Thieves — 20 waves
• Episode 2 · The Rival Lane — 30 waves
• Episode 3 · The Siege — 40 waves
• Endless Night — no end, rewards every 10 waves
• Events — limited-time modes like the Orchard Raid
Difficulties: **Normal → Hard → Nightmare**. Clear an episode on Normal to unlock the next one.

**🛡️ Defenders**
Rarities: Rare · Epic · Legendary · Mythic · Secret
Roles: melee, ranged, splash, support (buffs others), farm (earns cash), healer (repairs the gate)
Extra copies add stars (+5% damage each). Trait stones reroll traits.

**🎲 Summons**
50 lira per pull, 500 for 10. The banner changes every hour; its featured Legendary and Mythic take half of their rarity's chance.
Odds: Rare 75.5% · Epic 20% · Legendary 4% · Mythic 0.5%
Pity: a Legendary or better at least every **50** pulls, a Mythic at least every **400**.

**📅 Every day**
• Daily login reward — a 7-day streak (day 7: 120 lira + 200 qirsh). Missing a day resets it.
• #daily-challenge — a new challenge and question every day.
• Lane Diary (season pass) — 50 levels, XP from matches, dailies and codes.

**🎁 Codes** — open the Codes menu in the game. Active codes are in #codes.
**📱 Platforms** — phone first; PC, tablet and Xbox work too. Arabic and English.
