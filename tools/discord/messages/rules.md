# 📜 Rules · القوانين

**1. Be kind.** No insults, bullying, hate, or harassment. Disagree with ideas, not people.
**2. Keep it clean.** No adult, violent or shocking content, in text, images, names or avatars.
**3. You must be 13+** to use Discord ([Discord Terms](https://discord.com/terms)). Follow the [Roblox Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410) too.
**4. Protect yourself.** Never share passwords, cookies, phone numbers, addresses or real photos. Never click "free Robux" links or run files people send you. Staff will NEVER ask for your password.
**5. No scams, trading for real money, or selling accounts.** No begging for Robux or items.
**6. No exploits.** Don't share or advertise cheats. Found one? Report it privately in #private-report — real finds may earn the Bug Hunter role.
**7. Right channel, no spam.** No mass pings, no self-promotion outside your own posts, no off-server invites.
**8. Listen to staff.** Moderators can remove posts and time out or ban members. Questions about a decision → #help.

Breaking the rules: warning → timeout → ban, depending on how serious it is.

**١. كن لطيفاً** — لا شتائم ولا تنمّر. **٢.** لا محتوى غير لائق. **٣.** يجب أن يكون عمرك ١٣ سنة أو أكثر لاستخدام ديسكورد. **٤.** لا تشارك كلمة المرور أو معلوماتك الشخصية أبداً. **٥.** لا احتيال ولا بيع حسابات. **٦.** لا غش — أبلغ عن الثغرات في #private-report. **٧.** لا سبام. **٨.** اتبع تعليمات المشرفين.
