## 2. Core mechanics blueprint

**Lobby → match.** All gacha TDs use a hub place (summon shrine, index, shop, trading, time-chamber/AFK room) with map portals or elevators; TDS uses elevators with a countdown. Party sizes are 1–4 (some modes allow 1–6; Toilet TD scales boss HP with player count). Private matches/private servers are standard (Vanguards: Servers tab → private server with invite link). I could not find a published source for exact elevator countdown timings; TDS-style is ~30 s with vote-to-start-early. ([Vanguards private server guide](https://findingdulcinea.com/anime-vanguards-free-private-server/), [NamuWiki TTD](https://en.namu.wiki/w/Toilet%20Tower%20Defense))

**Modes and wave counts.**
- *Toilet TD:* Easy 20 waves (50 coins), Medium 30 (200), Hard 50 (400), Nightmare 50 (600); Easy boss at wave 20 with 38,500 HP; a loss on Nightmare still pays 15 coins (encourages fast retries). Endless exists (old wiki listed 110 waves, outdated). ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/roblox-toilet-tower-defense-get-coins-fast-tips-tricks/), [TTD wiki Boss Toilet](https://toilet-tower-defense-game.fandom.com/wiki/Boss_Toilet))
- *TDS:* Easy, Casual, Intermediate (Lv 5), Molten (Lv 15), Fallen, Frost + Hardcore/Voidcore and Challenge Trials; modes change spawns, wave count, wave bonus and rewards; Fallen health scaling kicks in at 3+ players. From memory (unverified — wiki blocked): Easy ~25, Casual/Intermediate ~30, Molten ~35, Fallen 40, Hardcore 50 waves; base HP ~100–150; starting cash ~$500; tower limit ~30–40 per player. ([TDS Gamemodes](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes), [v1.27.0 notes](https://breezewiki.discard.no/tds/wiki/V1.27.0))
- *Tower Defense X:* Easy, Intermediate, Elite, Expert, Endless, Nightmare, Ultra Nightmare, PvP; towers have HP past Easy. ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/TowerDefenseX))
- *Anime Vanguards:* Story (6 Acts per map; Act clears pay 80–240 gems), Infinite (unlocked after a map's 6 acts; rewards every 10th wave; wave 100 spike; enemies speed up each wave from 50; auto-skip force-enabled), Legend Stages, Raids (prep → defend → boss), Challenge (daily rotating restrictions: rarity limits, reduced placement limits, enemy buffs), Limited/event modes (rewards from wave 20, scaling caps at wave 100), Dungeons, Warfront, Level Editor. ([wiki.vanguards.gg Infinite](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Infinite_Mode), [Limited Modes](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Limited_Modes), [vanguards.wiki modes](https://vanguards.wiki/en/game-modes/anime-vanguards-game-modes/))

**Infinite reward curve (Vanguards, verified):** gems at wave 10/20/30 = 100/230/390 (+30 increment per tier until wave 50, then +5); gold 550/600/650 (+50 linear). Gem income ~1.7–2.1K/hour in Infinite per Pocket Tactics. ([Infinite Mode](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Infinite_Mode), [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-vanguards/codes))

**Placement rules.** Gacha TDs cap placements *per unit type* (e.g., Vanguards: Sprintwagon 3, Nami 1, Takaroda 1) rather than a flat per-player cap, plus range circles and ground/hill/cliff placement types (TDS has ground vs. cliff towers; TDX adds air/sea/land). Classic TDs use a per-player tower cap. ([bloxodes unit DB](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/anime-vanguards/units), [money units](https://www.animevanguardswiki.wiki/values/anime-vanguards-money-units))

**Upgrade tiers.** Vanguards units have 8–12 upgrade levels (Alocard base 8, evolved 12); example farm unit Sprintwagon: place 550 → U1 1,050 → … U4 3,050 → max total ~9,000; top DPS units list max-upgrade totals up to ~473,500 and ~20K damage/hit. Stats shown per unit: Damage, Range, SPA (seconds per attack), Hits/AoE, Placement cost, Placement count, Max upgrade cost. ([seemeta](https://seemeta.com/en/anime-vanguards/tier-list), [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/anime-vanguards/units))

**Enemy archetypes (consistent across TDS/TDX/anime TDs):** normal, fast, slow/tank, hidden/stealth (needs detection), flying (needs anti-air), shielded/armored (shield phase; e.g., mini-boss shield at 50% HP), lead/immune, splitters, healers, boss (every 5–10 waves, final boss on last wave). TDS Fallen 2025 numbers: Fallen Honor Guard 80,000 HP, Fallen Summoner 20,000, Fallen Shield 8,000. ([TDS Enemies](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Enemies), [Fallen Honor Guard](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Fallen_Honor_Guard))

**In-match economy.** Starting cash (TDS ~$500 unverified; Juggernaut challenge = Fallen + $300), per-wave income plus kill bounties; "farm"/money units (Vanguards Sprintwagon etc.) generate yen per wave and are the standard opening (2 farms then DPS). Speed: Vanguards has free 2x/auto-skip toggle; Anime Defenders gives F2P 2x and sells 3x (799 R$); ASTDX sells "Max Game Speed" (799 R$). ([AD Updates](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/Updates), [ASTDX Game Passes](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Passes), [auto-skip how-to](https://allthings.how/anime-vanguards-how-to-skip-waves-auto-skip-waves-setting/))

**End-of-match rewards:** gems + gold/coins + account XP (+ unit XP/shards/materials; Vanguards gives map-specific "XP food"). Toilet TD: 50/200/400/600 coins by difficulty. Leveling gates content (Vanguards: Lv 10 for Secret/Shiny units, Lv 30 guilds, Lv 50 trading; TDS: Lv 5 Intermediate, Lv 15 Molten).

## 3. Unit economy / gacha

**Summon costs & rotation.** Standard: **50 gems per summon, 40 with VIP** (Vanguards, Anime Royale, Anime Adventures); Anime Last Stand uses 75/750 on some banners. Banners **rotate hourly** (Vanguards special/event banners at :00; ASTDX Banner X at :00 and Banner Y at :30; Anime Defenders hourly "alongside challenges"); Vanguards "Selection" banner lets you pin a Mythic for 24 h. ([wiki.vanguards.gg Summon](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Summon), [ASTDX Summon](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Summon), [AD Banner](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/Banner))

**Odds tables (verified where noted).**
- *Anime Vanguards (wiki, current):* Rare 75.496% / Epic 20% / Legendary 4% / Mythic-Exclusive 0.5% / Vanguard Memoria 0.0075% / Vanguard unit 0.005%; Shiny 1.5% per unit (3% with Shiny Hunter pass). Pity: Legendary 50 summons (2,500 gems; 2,000 VIP), Mythic 400 (20,000 gems), Vanguard 25,000 (1.25M gems); Legendary/Mythic pity resets on hit, Vanguard pity is a one-time counter. Luck boosts Super (99 R$) / Ultra (199 R$) last 1 h, stack to +62.5%, do not touch Secret/Vanguard/shiny odds.
- *ASTDX:* 3★ 27% / 4★ 8% / 5★ 1% / Unbound 0.01%; 5★ pity 100, Unbound pity 2,000.
- *Anime Defenders:* Rare ~82.5 (or 27.5 in some guides) / Epic 12.25–15.25 / Legendary 2 / Mythic 0.25 / Secret 0.01%; pity 50 Legendary / 400 Mythic; limited-banner pity expires with the banner.
- *Anime Last Stand:* Rare 70 / Epic 27.5 / Legendary 2 / Mythic 0.5%; pity 60 Legendary / 250 Mythic.
- *Toilet TD crates:* 300 gems; e.g., 50/40/9/1% or 50/35/13/2%; dev crate 0.1% (Telanthric).
Sources: [vanguards.gg](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Summon), [ASTDX](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Summon), [GINX ALS](https://www.ginx.tv/en/roblox/anime-last-stand-summon-chances-pity-system), [Gamezebo AD pity](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/anime-defenders-pity-guide/), [Sportskeeda TTD crates](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/toilet-tower-defense-forcefield-units-crate-odds).

**Rarity naming:** Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic (→ Exclusive/Secret → Vanguard/"Unbound"). Toilet TD uses Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary/Mythic/Godly…; ASTDX uses 3★–5★ + Unbound. Any naming works if the colour ramp is recognisable (blue/purple/gold/red/rainbow).

**Traits, shiny, evolution, shards.** Trait rerolls consume Trait Crystals/Rerolls (earned from Infinite 50+, codes, battle pass — Vanguards pass gives 180–1,020 rerolls/season); traits are tiered with their own pity thresholds. Shiny = cosmetic + stat variant (1.5%). Evolution requires farmed materials from raids/dungeons; Vanguards adds "Memoria" (equippable cards) with their own banners. Unit storage is capped and sold: Vanguards +50 slots for 149 R$; Anime Defenders "More Booth Space" +100 for 99 R$. ([trait rerolls](https://vanguards2ndanniversary-wiki.wiki/guides/trait-rerolls/), [u7buy AV passes](https://www.u7buy.com/anime-vanguards/anime-vanguards-gamepass), [AD store](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/In-Game_Store))

**Trading.** Vanguards: trading returned Nov 10 2025, unlocked at Lv 50; tradeable = limited units, skins, familiars, giftables; traits/pity reset on trade; trade bans for duping. Toilet TD's economy spawned external value lists (Telanthric Titan "3.8B gems") and scams: "signing units" fake-partner scam, double-trade, fake middlemen, dupe promises. Dev-side mitigations discussed on DevForum: lock both inventories during trade, server-side atomic swap, session locking (ProfileStore) to prevent dupe via multi-server. ([stealthygaming AV trading](https://stealthygaming.com/anime-vanguards-trading-guide/), [TTD Trading](https://toilet-tower-defense-game.fandom.com/wiki/Trading), [DevForum duping](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/preventing-duping-methods-in-trading/2688896), [TechTimes scams](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/312739/20251117/roblox-trading-guide-2025-how-trade-items-safely-avoid-scams-online.htm))

**Earning gems (F2P loop).** Story stage clears (80–240), Infinite milestones (100/230/390… per 10 waves), raids (~30/clear), daily login streak, daily/weekly quests, free battle-pass track (Vanguards: 50K gems/season), AFK "Time Chamber"/time-machine, codes (Vanguards "MiniUpd2" = 50,000 gems + 200 rerolls), mailbox gifts, group-join bonus. Toilet TD was gem-stingy (daily quest 5 gems, weekly 20) and sold gems directly. ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-vanguards/codes), [Beebom codes](https://beebom.com/anime-vanguards-codes/), [VGC gems](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/anime-vanguards-gem-farming/), [PocketGamer TTD gems](https://www.pocketgamer.com/roblox/how-to-get-gems-toilet-tower-defense))

**Roblox Paid Random Items (PRI) policy — must comply.** Anything random bought with Robux *or with in-game currency purchasable with Robux* (gems) is a PRI: summons, crates, trait rerolls bought with Robux-currency, luck boosts, pity. Requirements: show every outcome with numerical % totaling 100% before purchase (grouping identical odds is OK; long lists behind a labelled "Info/Details" button, not a bare (i)); odds must update dynamically while luck boosts/pity are active and when one-time outcomes are exhausted; call `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync()` and for `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted == true` either offer a free earnable path, fixed-order outcomes, direct EV-priced sale, hide/block the generator, or keep the player out of that area; if `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed == false` block trading of PRI outcomes. Regions are not listed in docs (policy is per-user; UK under-18 and South Korea rules drove the 2026 global tightening). A DevForum reply claims TDS does not use the API yet blocks countries — unverified. ([Creator Docs PRI](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items), [DevForum clarification](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarifying-requirements-for-paid-random-items/4654622), [TechTimes Korea](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319148/20260626/koreas-loot-box-rules-push-roblox-disclose-item-odds-worldwide.htm))

## 5. Retention and trending tactics

- **Update cadence:** Vanguards ships major x.0 updates every 3–5 weeks with x.5 mid-patches; each x.0 brings a new season pass, 3–5 units, an event map and a code. TDS/TDX run seasonal events with passes. Weekly hotfix + "update countdown" in the lobby (title "[UPDATE in 3 DAYS]") is standard practice; Plants & Brainrots used weekly live "Admin Abuse" events. ([allthings.how AV cadence](https://allthings.how/anime-vanguards-2nd-anniversary-update-features-and-release-status/), [games.gg P&B](https://games.gg/news/plants-vs-brainrots-roblox-viral-hit/))
- **Codes as marketing:** released on update day, like-milestones (e.g., "new code at 55K likes"), Discord member counts ("3K server members"), CCU milestones ("3K CCU"), bug-fix apologies ("SorryForShutdown"); announced via Discord, X, YouTube, TikTok; typically expire in 1–4 weeks; code panel often gated by level (AD: Lv 8). Guide sites (Pocket Tactics, Beebom, Dexerto, Sportskeeda) republish codes monthly — free SEO traffic. ([Pocket Tactics Slop TD](https://www.pockettactics.com/slop-tower-defense-codes), [Dexerto TDS](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tower-defense-simulator-tds-codes-3349205/), [Destructoid TDX](https://www.destructoid.com/tower-defense-x-codes/))
- **First-session hooks:** choose-your-starter (Vanguards: pick 1 of 3 iconic units), free gems/tickets on tutorial pop-ups, daily login streak with front-loaded rewards, mailbox gifts, guaranteed early pity (Legendary at 50 pulls ≈ 2,500 gems, reachable in day 1–2). ([Sportskeeda AV beginner](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/anime-vanguards-a-beginner-s-guide))
- **Loops:** daily/weekly quests (reset timers), AFK rooms (time chamber), leaderboards (Infinite; Vanguards removed Infinite leaderboards Feb 2026), guilds (Lv 30, 20 members), seasonal events with event currency + limited units, "Secret" units at 0.005–0.01% as social-media bait, trading as an end-game.
- **Streamer/YouTuber seeding:** TDX is founder-YouTuber; anime TDs seed via early-access to Discord boosters (Anime Royale), content-creator codes, and "showcase" videos; update trailers on YouTube with countdown.
- **Store presentation:** title convention `[🔥UPDATE 3 + CODE] Game Name` / `[NEW UNIT] …`; icon = one big character + 1–2 words, must read at 128×128; thumbnails rotate per update. Roblox supports native thumbnail A/B tests (avg +8.5% qPTR with personalization) but **not icon A/B** — rotate icons sequentially and compare. ([creatorxp icons](https://creatorxp.gg/guides/roblox-game-icon-mistakes), [DevForum icon A/B request](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/icon-ab-testing/3274267))
- **Arabic/MENA lever:** Roblox launched Arabic platform-wide Feb 2025 (WGS Dubai); DAU and hours across Egypt, KSA, Morocco, Qatar, UAE grew ~80%/76% per year on average Q3'21→Q3'24 (Roblox/Access Partnership report); KSA DAU quadrupled over 5 years; Saudi creator earnings +59% YoY; >50K developers in UAE; Niko: 72M gamers in MENA-3 (2024) → 84.3M by 2029. Caveats: in-experience chat disabled in KSA/most of the Middle East since Sep 2025 pending Arabic moderation; Egypt reportedly blocked Roblox. Implication: full Arabic (RTL) localization of UI, unit names and store text plus text-free thumbnails is cheap and under-served; but design for chat-off servers (emotes/pings). ## 6. Technical patterns

- **Server-authoritative sim, client renders.** Server owns enemy HP, progress-along-path, tower targeting and cash; clients never move enemies authoritatively. The proven cheap approach: server sends *spawn events* (enemy id, type, path index, spawn time, speed), clients advance enemies deterministically along the same waypoint list, server sends periodic corrections (one thread used 20 s) and explicit events for stun/slow/speed changes/death. One DevForum case cut recv from ~250 KB/s to ~75 KB/s with 200 enemies just by using array indices instead of string keys and sending only Y-rotation; sending path *numbers* instead of path data cut per-update payload from ~10 KB to <1 KB. ([DevForum recv thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defense-game-has-high-recv-network-traffic-performance-issue/2517970), [server/client enemies](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defence-enemies-serverclient/1858587))
- **One loop, no per-enemy scripts.** Keep enemies in a table and advance all in a single `RunService.Heartbeat` (or `PostSimulation`) with dt; no `while true` per enemy; target search via distance checks on cached positions (optionally every N frames). Clients lerp/tween between states. ([single-loop thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defense-enemy-movement-system-glitchy/2221419), [movement tutorial 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/beginner-intermediate-creating-an-enemy-movement-system-for-a-tower-defense-game/3836680), [target search perf](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defense-target-searching-performance-impact/2456962))
- **Replication channel.** A single RemoteEvent (or UnreliableRemoteEvent for position snapshots) carrying packed arrays/buffers; attributes on a folder for low-frequency state (wave number, base HP); pooled enemy models on the client (clone once, reuse); Y-rotation only; bit-pack ints. Libraries mentioned: FastNet2/ByteNet-style packers.
- **Places.** Hub place (lobby, summon, trade) + match place; matches run in **reserved servers** via `TeleportService:ReserveServer()` + `TeleportAsync`/`TeleportToPrivateServer` with `TeleportOptions` carrying party/map/difficulty; retry loop (one dev: up to 5 tries, 1 s apart); teleport does not work in Studio (test in a single place with a debug flag); loadout/unit data should be re-read from DataStore in the match place rather than trusted from teleport data. ([elevator thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defense-game-elevator/1452155), [Tower Defenders teleport](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defenders-feedback-on-teleport-system/624784), [data transfer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tower-defense-data-transfer/3681263))
- **Data.** ProfileStore (successor to ProfileService) for inventory/currency: session locking prevents dupes across servers/teleports, auto-save, reconciliation of new template fields, retry/rate-limit handling; buffer-encode units if inventories get large. ([DevForum ProfileStore](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/whats-the-advantages-of-using-profilestore/4459941), [kitsblox ProfileStore](https://kitsblox.com/blog/roblox-save-system-profilestore))
- **Anti-exploit basics.** Validate every placement/upgrade/sell on the server (ownership, cash, placement cap, range, position on valid ground within N studs of player cursor hit), rate-limit remotes, never accept client damage/kill events, compute rewards server-side, keep PRI odds and purchases server-side with `MarketplaceService.ProcessReceipt` idempotency keys, lock inventories during trades.
- **Lobby matchmaking:** elevators/portals with a server-side countdown (start early on vote), capacity 1–4, difficulty vote, then reserve server; show "Searching…" and fall back to solo if teleport fails.
