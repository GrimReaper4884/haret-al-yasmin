## 1. The brief (send this part)

**Project.** *Haret Al-Yasmin* ("Jasmine Lane") is a family-friendly tower-defense game on Roblox, set in an
old Damascus neighbourhood in the 1930s. The lane's residents defend its wooden gate at night. We need short,
original Levantine music cues and a few sound effects. They must work on phone speakers (mono-compatible,
little sub-bass) and loop cleanly where marked.

**Style.** Classical Levantine/Damascene instrumental: oud, qanun, nay, riqq, darbuka and frame drums, with
bowed strings for the dramatic cues. Real maqam intonation (quarter-tones in Bayati and Rast) is the reason
we are hiring a player rather than using a sample library.

**What we must not receive (hard rules).**

1. Nothing from, or resembling, any TV series' theme or score, especially the Damascus "hara" dramas
   (*Bab al-Hara*, *Ayyam Shamiyeh*, *Al-Khawali* and similar). No quoted melody, no recognisable motif, no
   "in the style of the title song". Roblox fingerprints uploads automatically, and a match removes the
   sound and can strike the account.
2. No samples, loops or recordings you do not own or have a licence to sublicense. If you use a sample
   library, name it and its licence.
3. No vocals with words. Wordless calls and choir are fine.
4. No AI-generated material unless you tell us in advance and we agree in writing.
5. Do not register the cues with Content ID, YouTube, Audible Magic or any other fingerprinting or
   distribution service, now or later. If you do, our own game and trailers get claimed.

### Batch 1: signature cues (start here)

| Cue | Length | Brief |
|-----|--------|-------|
| `gate_close` | ~2 s | **Foley, not music.** Two heavy wooden gate leaves thud shut, iron rings rattle, then a thick wooden bar drops into iron hooks (wood on wood, low). Dry, close-miked. This is the signature sound of the game. Please deliver 3 takes. |
| `shabriyeh` | ~1 s | A short curved blade drawn from its sheath: a metallic "shing", immediately followed by **one hard qanun chord**. Dry, sharp, **no reverb tail**. Used for the special ability and for rare reveals. |
| `lobby` | 2–3 min, **seamless loop** | Daytime in the lane. Maqam **Bayati**, 80–90 bpm. Solo oud taqsim over a soft qanun ostinato, light riqq brushes. Warm, unhurried, a little nostalgic. |
| `summon_reveal` | ~0.5 s | One warm oud strum or chord. Please deliver **4 variants** (different chords or voicings). |
| `defeat` | ~5 s | A slow descending **Bayati** phrase on solo oud that ends unresolved. Sad, not tragic. |

### Delivery

- **Masters:** 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV, peaks ≤ −1 dBTP, one file per cue or take, named
  `<cueId>_<take>.wav` (e.g. `gate_close_2.wav`).
- **Game files:** the same cues as OGG Vorbis (q6 or higher) or 320 kbps MP3, each under 20 MB.
- **Loops:** the loop points are the file's start and end. No silence at either end, and any reverb tail
  folded back into the start so the loop is seamless. Please send a 3× looped preview MP3 so we can check
  the seam.
- **Mono check:** each cue must still sound right summed to mono.
- **Stems** (optional, appreciated for loops): one stem per instrument group.
- **Notes:** a short text with maqam, tempo, instruments, players, studio, and any sample library used.

### Process and timeline

1. You send a 20–30 s sketch of `lobby` and one take of `gate_close`. We approve or give one round of notes.
2. You deliver Batch 1 (two revision rounds included).
3. You deliver Batch 2 (two revision rounds included).
4. Payment: 30 % on sketch approval, 40 % on Batch 1, 30 % on Batch 2.

## 2. Rights clause (plain language, NOT legal advice)

> **Have a lawyer review this before signing. It is a plain-language draft for discussion only.**
> The goal: the owner can use, edit and loop the cues anywhere, forever, including trailers and store
> videos outside Roblox (which the v1 licensed library does **not** allow).

The musician agrees that, on payment of each milestone:

1. **Assignment.** All copyright and related rights in the delivered cues, takes, stems and sketches
   (the "Works"), including the recordings and the underlying compositions, are assigned to
   Teyam Hamoud, worldwide, for the full term of copyright. Where the law does not allow
   assignment, the musician grants an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence
   with the right to sublicense.
2. **Use.** The owner may use, edit, cut, loop, remix, combine with other material, and publish the Works
   in any game, trailer, advertisement, video or social post, on any platform, without further payment.
3. **Moral rights.** To the extent the law allows, the musician waives or agrees not to assert moral
   rights (for example, the right to object to edits). Credit is given as
   "Original music by <musician's name>" in the game's credits where practical; missing credit is not a breach.
4. **Originality warranty.** The Works are the musician's own original creation. They do not copy or
   imitate any existing work, in particular any TV series' theme or score. Every third-party sample is
   identified and licensed for this use. No AI-generated material is included unless disclosed and agreed in
   writing.
5. **No fingerprint registration.** The musician will not register the Works, or anything containing
   them, with Content ID, Audible Magic, distributors or any similar service. If a claim arises because of
   such a registration, the musician will release it promptly.
6. **Performers.** If other players perform, the musician obtains their written consent and assignment on
   the same terms and pays them.
7. **Portfolio.** The musician may play up to 30 s of the Works in a personal portfolio after the game's
   public release, without naming unreleased features.

## 3. Acceptance checklist (per cue, done by us)

Matches `AUDIO_BIBLE.md` §4.2. A cue is accepted only when every box is ticked.

## 5. Where to post it [OWNER: choose]

- **Marketplaces with contracts and escrow:** SoundBetter (search "oud", "qanun", "Arabic session
  musician"), Fiverr (gigs for "oud player", "Arabic music"), AirGigs, Upwork. Ask for a portfolio with
  **solo taqsim** recordings, not only library-style "Arabian" tracks.
- **Levantine music community:** graduates and teachers of Levantine conservatories (for example Damascus,
  Beirut and Amman music institutes, and the Syrian diaspora scenes in Berlin and Istanbul). Reach them
  through faculty pages or music-school notice boards, never through private contacts found online.
- **Foley** (`gate_close`, `gate_hit`, `ui_click`, `coin`) can go to a separate sound designer on the same
  marketplaces, or be recorded at home on a real wooden door with a decent recorder. Note the recordist in
  the sidecar either way.

Screening questions to ask every candidate:

1. Can you play Bayati with its neutral second, and Hijaz? Send a 30 s taqsim.
2. Do you record live (where?) or use sample libraries (which)?
3. Will you sign a full copyright assignment with no Content ID registration?
4. Have you ever had a track claimed or removed for copyright?
